How I think

Bottleneck first. Dashboard second.

1

Identify the business bottleneck

Clarify what is slowing growth, conversion, efficiency or decision quality.

2

Quantify the impact

Size the opportunity or leakage before going deep on data cuts.

3

Analyze patterns and trade-offs

Separate signal from noise across cohorts, stages and operating constraints.

4

Prioritize high-leverage actions

5

Translate analysis into owner-ready actions and KPI tracking.
